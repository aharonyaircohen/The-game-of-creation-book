# Laws of the Game — Working Draft v1.0

> Status: Working model. This is the current conceptual framework, not a final canonical list.
> Purpose: Explain the game model clearly through four layers of reality.

## 1. Atzilut — The Creator / The One

The deepest layer: reality before separation into individual parts.

### 1. Law of Oneness
Everything exists within one unified system. Separation appears within the game, but does not cancel the underlying unity.

### 2. Law of Potentiality
The whole contains more possibilities than those that are actually manifested. Manifest reality is one expression within a wider field of potential.

---

## 2. Beriah — The Player

The layer in which potential becomes a bounded field of possibilities available to a particular player.

### 3. Law of Bounded Possibility
The player does not initially have access to the entire field of potential. At every stage, a limited set of possibilities is available.

### 4. Law of Choice
From the possibilities currently available, a path is selected. Choice moves the game from possibility toward a particular direction.

---

## 3. Yetzirah — The Game Engine

The layer of laws that determines how states interact and change.

### 5. Law of Difference / Polarity
For anything to happen, there must be a distinction between states. Difference enables information, tension, flow and movement. Positive and negative polarity are one expression of this principle.

### 6. Law of Transformation / Causality
One state can become another, but the transition is not arbitrary. Change follows the rules of the game. This includes the principle of cause and effect.

### 7. Law of Oscillation / Frequency
When change is periodic, it produces oscillation, rhythm and frequency. Waves and many cyclic phenomena arise from this principle.

### 8. Law of Resonance
A system does not respond equally to every input. It can respond more strongly to patterns or frequencies that match its structure.

### 9. Law of Symmetry / Consistency
The engine does not change its rules arbitrarily. Under equivalent conditions, the same underlying rules remain consistent, making the game stable and learnable.

---

## 4. Assiyah — The Character / Physical World

The layer in which the laws of the engine become limited, concrete experience.

### 10. Law of Manifestation / Constraint
For a possibility to become actual within the game, it must take a defined and limited form. Body, location, resources and boundaries are expressions of this principle.

### 11. Law of Relativity
Within manifested reality, measurements and experiences arise through relationships and frames of reference rather than from an absolute viewpoint. Time, space and motion acquire meaning within these relations.

### 12. Law of Conservation
Within the physical layer, change remains constrained by consistent conservation rules. Certain quantities are preserved across interactions and transformations.

---

## The Flow of the Game

**Atzilut:** The whole contains potential.  
**Beriah:** The player receives a bounded field of possibilities and chooses within it.  
**Yetzirah:** The game engine processes change according to its laws.  
**Assiyah:** The result becomes a limited, physical experience for the character.

## What Is Not Classified as a Fundamental Law

The following currently belong to other parts of the model rather than this list of fundamental laws:

- Consciousness — part of the architecture of the model.
- The player — an entity in the model.
- Rendering — a mechanism.
- Time and space — structures of manifested experience.
- The body — the character's interface.
- Mirrors, responsibility, victimhood and development — principles of playing and inner development, to be treated separately.
