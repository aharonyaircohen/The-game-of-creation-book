# Laws of the Game — Working Draft v1.1

> Status: Working model. This is the current conceptual framework, not a final canonical list.
> Purpose: Explain the fundamental laws of the game clearly through four layers of reality.

## 1. Atzilut — The Creator / The One

The deepest layer: reality before separation into individual parts.

### 1. Law of Oneness
Everything exists within one unified system. Separation appears within the game, but does not cancel the underlying unity.

### 2. Law of Potentiality
The whole contains more possibilities than those that are actually manifested. Manifest reality is one expression within a wider field of potential.

---

## 2. Beriah — The Player

The layer in which the total potential becomes a bounded field of possibilities available to a particular player.

### 3. Law of Bounded Possibility
The player does not have access to the entire field of potential at once. At every stage, only a limited set of possibilities is available.

### 4. Law of Choice
From the possibilities currently available, the player selects a direction. Choice moves the game from possibility toward a particular path.

---

## 3. Yetzirah — The Game Engine

The layer of laws that determines how states, information and interactions behave.

### 5. Law of Information and State
At every moment, the game has a state defined by information. The physical world is an expression of that state, and change in reality is change in the state of the system.

### 6. Law of Difference / Polarity
For information, tension, flow or movement to exist, there must be a distinction between states. Positive and negative polarity are one expression of this deeper principle.

### 7. Law of Transformation / Causality
A state can become another state, but the transition is not arbitrary. Change follows the rules of the game, and each new state changes which possibilities can follow.

### 8. Law of Oscillation / Frequency / Resonance
When change is periodic, it produces oscillation, rhythm and frequency. Systems can respond more strongly to patterns or frequencies that fit their structure.

### 9. Law of Symmetry / Consistency
The engine does not change its rules arbitrarily. Under equivalent conditions, the same underlying rules remain consistent, making the game stable and learnable.

---

## 4. Assiyah — The Character / Physical World

The layer in which the laws of the engine become limited, concrete experience.

### 10. Law of Manifestation / Constraint
For a possibility to become actual within the game, it must take a defined and limited form. Body, location, resources and boundaries are expressions of this principle.

### 11. Law of Perspective / Relativity
Every character experiences a local and partial view of the larger system. Measurements, time, motion and experience depend on relationships and frames of reference.

### 12. Law of Conservation
Within the physical layer, change remains constrained by consistent conservation rules. Certain quantities are preserved across interactions and transformations.

---

## What Is Not Classified Here as a Fundamental Law

The following belong to other parts of the model rather than this list of fundamental laws:

- Consciousness — part of the architecture of the model.
- The player — an entity in the model.
- Rendering — a mechanism.
- Time and space — structures of manifested experience.
- The body — the character's interface.
- Mirrors, responsibility, victimhood, development, attention and life lessons — principles of playing and inner development, to be treated separately.
- The transition between dimensions — part of the architecture and operation of the model, not a fundamental physical law.
